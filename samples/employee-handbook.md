# Fernhill Labs Employee Handbook

Fernhill Labs is a fictional 60-person software company. This handbook is sample
data for demoing Lorvo; none of these policies are real.

## Time Off

### Vacation Allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued
monthly (1.67 days per month). Part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata.
Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year; anything above that expires
on March 31.

### Requesting Leave

Request vacation in the **PeopleHub** portal under *Time Off > New Request*.
Submit requests at least **2 weeks in advance** for absences of 3 days or more,
and at least 2 business days in advance for shorter absences. Your manager
approves or declines within 3 business days. Once approved, add the dates to the
shared team calendar.

During the December code freeze (December 15 to January 2), requests longer than
3 days need approval from your department head as well as your manager.

### Sick Days

You have 10 paid sick days per year; they do not carry over. If you are sick,
message your manager before 9:30am local time and log the day in PeopleHub
afterwards. For absences longer than 3 consecutive working days, provide a
doctor's note to people@fernhill.example.

### Public Holidays

Fernhill follows the public holidays of the country you are employed in. If you
work on a public holiday because of an on-call rotation, you receive a
replacement day off to be taken within 3 months.

### Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 18 weeks of fully paid leave; non-birthing parents
receive 10 weeks. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected due date and
must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Tell People Ops at
least 8 weeks before your planned start date.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone.
Outside core hours, schedule your day as you like.

### Remote Work

Fernhill is remote-friendly. You may work from anywhere in your country of
employment. Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar
year requires approval from People Ops because of tax and insurance rules.

### Home Office Stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750 for a desk, chair,
monitor or similar equipment. After that, you get $300 per year for
replacements. Claim it as an expense with the category "Home office".

## Expenses

### What Can Be Expensed

Reasonable costs incurred for work are reimbursed: travel booked through
TripDesk, meals while travelling (up to $60 per day), conference tickets
approved by your manager, and books or courses up to the learning budget.

### Submitting Expenses

Submit expenses in **Spendly** within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an
itemised receipt for anything over $25. Reimbursements are paid with the next
monthly payroll run if approved before the 20th of the month.

### Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,200 for courses, books and
conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment and Security

Company laptops are managed by IT. Do not disable disk encryption or the
endpoint protection agent. Report a lost or stolen device to
security@fernhill.example within one hour. See the IT Security Policy (PDF) for
details on passwords and data handling.

## Leaving Fernhill

The notice period is one month for employees in their first year and two months
afterwards. Return your laptop and any other equipment within 10 days of your
last day. Unused vacation days are paid out with your final salary.
