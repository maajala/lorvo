Fernhill Labs Office FAQ (fictional sample data)

Where is the office? Our only office is at 12 Harbour Street, Unit 4. It is open
from 8am to 7pm on weekdays. Anyone may work from it; no booking needed for hot
desks, but meeting rooms must be booked in the Rooms app.

How do I get into the building? Your badge is mailed to you in your first week.
If you forget it, the front desk can issue a day pass with photo ID.

Is there parking? There are 10 bike spaces in the courtyard and no car parking.
The nearest public garage offers a 20% discount with your Fernhill badge.

What is the guest Wi-Fi? Connect to "Fernhill-Guest". The password is posted on
the kitchen noticeboard and changes on the first Monday of each month.

Can I bring my dog? Dogs are welcome on Fridays if they are friendly and
house-trained. Register your dog with the office manager first.

Who do I contact about the office? Email office@fernhill.example or post in the
#office Slack channel.
